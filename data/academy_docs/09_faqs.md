## Do you teach kids?
Yes, we teach students of all ages, including young children. Noorani Qaida and Masnoon Duas are especially popular starting points for kids.

Yes, we have both male and female tutors. You can request a female tutor for your daughter or for sisters at the time of enrollment.

## Can I switch tutors if I'm not comfortable?
Yes, you can request a tutor change at any time, free of charge, including switching between male and female tutors.

## Is the free trial really free? Do I need to enter card details?
Yes, the 3-day trial is completely free and no card or payment details are required to start.

If you're satisfied, you can continue with our standard monthly plan at PKR 5,000/month. There's no obligation to continue.
