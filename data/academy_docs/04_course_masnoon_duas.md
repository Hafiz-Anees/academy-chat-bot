# Course: Masnoon Duas (Daily Supplications from the Sunnah)

**What it is:** A course teaching authentic daily supplications (duas) that the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) recited and taught, for everyday situations, along with their meanings and correct pronunciation.

**Who it's for:** Students of all ages, especially recommended for children as part of their basic Islamic upbringing, and adults who want to learn or revise essential daily duas.

**What's covered:**
- Duas for daily routines (waking up, sleeping, eating, entering/leaving home, etc.)
- Duas for worship (before/after prayer, wudu, entering masjid)
- Short Surahs commonly memorized alongside duas
- Correct pronunciation and meaning of each dua
- Etiquette (Adab) of making dua

**Duration:** Flexible — can be a short standalone course (a few weeks) or taught alongside Noorani Qaida/Hifz classes.

**Class format:** One-on-one online sessions, often combined with other courses for younger students.

**Prerequisites:** None — suitable for complete beginners including young children.
