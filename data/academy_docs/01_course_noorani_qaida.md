# Course: Noorani Qaida

**What it is:** Noorani Qaida is the foundational course for students who are completely new to reading the Quran. It teaches the Arabic alphabet, correct pronunciation (makharij), joining letters, and basic reading rules needed before a student can begin reading the Quran itself.

**Who it's for:** Absolute beginners of any age — especially young children starting their Quran journey, or adults who never learned to read Arabic script.

**What's covered:**
- Arabic alphabet recognition and pronunciation (Makharij-ul-Huroof)
- Harakat (short vowels: Fatha, Kasra, Damma)
- Joining letters to form words
- Basic Tajweed rules introduced gradually
- Reading practice leading up to starting the Quran

**Duration:** Typically completed in 2–4 months depending on the student's pace and class frequency.

**Class format:** One-on-one online classes, live with a tutor.

**Prerequisites:** None — this is the starting point for every new student who cannot yet read Quranic Arabic.

**After completing Noorani Qaida:** Students move on to Quran reading, followed by Tajweed and/or Hifz, based on their goals.
