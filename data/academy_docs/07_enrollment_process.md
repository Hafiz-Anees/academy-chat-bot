# How to Enroll — Step by Step

1. **Contact us** via WhatsApp, Instagram, or Messenger with the student's name, age, and course of interest.
2. **Book your free 3-day trial** — tell us your preferred class timing/timezone and tutor gender preference (male/female).
3. **Attend the trial classes** — the tutor will assess reading level and recommend the right starting point (Noorani Qaida, Quran reading, Tajweed, or Hifz).
4. **Choose a monthly plan** — standard fee is PKR 5,000/month (converted for international students).
5. **Make payment** via bank transfer, JazzCash/Easypaisa, or [international payment method].
6. **Regular classes begin** — you'll receive a fixed weekly schedule with your assigned tutor.

**Information we'll need from you:**
- Student's full name 
- Preferred course
- email address
- Phone number
