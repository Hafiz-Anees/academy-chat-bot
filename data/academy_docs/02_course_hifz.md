# Course: Hifz-ul-Quran (Quran Memorization)

**What it is:** A structured memorization program to help students memorize the Holy Quran with correct pronunciation and Tajweed, along with regular revision (Dohrani/Sabaq-Sabqi-Manzil system) to retain what's memorized.

**Who it's for:** Students who can already read the Quran fluently (i.e., have completed Noorani Qaida and basic Quran reading) and wish to memorize part or all of the Quran.

**What's covered:**
- New memorization (Sabaq) — daily assigned portion
- Recent revision (Sabqi) — reinforcing recently memorized portions
- Old revision (Manzil) — long-term retention of previously memorized Quran
- Correction of Tajweed mistakes during memorization

**Duration:** Varies by student — full Hifz typically takes 2–5 years depending on age, daily class frequency, and pace. Partial Hifz (specific Surahs/Juz) available too.

**Class format:** One-on-one online sessions, ideally daily or at least 5 days/week for consistent progress.

**Prerequisites:** Must be able to read the Quran with reasonable fluency. If not, we recommend starting with Noorani Qaida / Quran reading first.

**Note:** Hifz requires strong consistency — students/parents should be ready for daily practice outside of class time as well.
