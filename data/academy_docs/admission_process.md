# Admission Process

## Overview

Our admission process is simple and designed to help students enroll in the appropriate course or grade based on their educational background.

## Eligibility

Students who have successfully completed **5th grade or above** are eligible to apply for admission to our academy.

## Required Information

Applicants must provide the following details when submitting an admission request:

- Full Name
- Email Address
- Phone Number
- Program or Grade
- Previous Education (optional)
- Additional Notes (optional)

## Admission Steps

1. Submit your admission request with the required information.
2. Our admissions team will review your application.
3. We will contact you via phone or email.
4. A counseling session or assessment may be scheduled if required.
5. After approval, you will receive enrollment instructions and class details.

## FAQs

### Can I apply online?

- Currently, online admission is **not available**. Please contact our academy or visit our campus to complete the admission process.

### Who can apply?

- Any student who has completed **5th grade or higher** is eligible to apply.

### What information is required?

- Full Name
- Email Address
- Phone Number
- Program or Grade

### How will I know if my admission is accepted?

- Our admissions team will contact you via your provided phone number or email after reviewing your application.
