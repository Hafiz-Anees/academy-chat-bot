# Course: Tajweed

**What it is:** A course focused specifically on the rules of correct Quranic pronunciation and recitation, ensuring the Quran is recited exactly as it was revealed, with proper articulation points and rules.

**Who it's for:** Students who can already read the Quran and want to refine their recitation, remove reading mistakes, and learn to recite beautifully and correctly. Also recommended for those preparing for Hifz.

**What's covered:**
- Makharij-ul-Huroof (correct articulation points of Arabic letters)
- Rules of Noon Sakinah and Tanween (Izhar, Idgham, Iqlab, Ikhfa)
- Rules of Meem Sakinah
- Madd (elongation) rules and their durations
- Qalqalah, Ghunnah, and other essential Tajweed rules
- Waqf (stopping rules) and correct pausing while reciting

**Duration:** Typically 6 months to 1 year for a solid foundation, though it can be studied at the student's own pace.

**Class format:** One-on-one online classes with live correction and practice.

**Prerequisites:** Basic ability to read the Quran (completion of Noorani Qaida recommended).

**Who benefits most:** Anyone wanting to improve recitation quality — whether for personal worship, leading prayer, or as preparation for Hifz.
