# Quantiro — AI Automation & Agentic AI

## Overview

Quantiro provides AI automation and agentic AI solutions for businesses.

These solutions are designed around the specific requirements, workflows, and objectives of each client.

Quantiro does not have a fixed public price for AI automation or agentic AI services.

## Confirmed AI Services

The following AI-related offerings are confirmed Quantiro services:

* AI automation
* Agentic AI
* AI chat agents
* AI voice agents
* AI-based applications

Each solution can be customized according to the client's requirements.

## AI Automation

AI automation involves using AI-based systems to automate business tasks or workflows.

The exact automation solution depends on the client's requirements.

Possible areas for automation may include business workflows, customer interactions, information processing, and repetitive tasks.

However, the AI assistant must not claim that a specific automation, integration, platform, or workflow is supported unless it is explicitly documented in the approved Quantiro knowledge base.

## Agentic AI

Quantiro provides agentic AI solutions for businesses.

Agentic AI systems can be designed to perform tasks and interact with business workflows according to defined objectives and rules.

The exact capabilities of an agentic AI solution depend on the project requirements and implementation.

The AI assistant must not promise specific capabilities, integrations, autonomy levels, tools, or actions unless they are explicitly documented.

## AI Chat Agents

Quantiro provides AI-based chat agents.

Chat agents can be developed according to a business's requirements and intended use case.

The exact features, platforms, integrations, knowledge sources, and workflows depend on the agreed project scope.

The assistant must not assume that a particular messaging platform, CRM, database, API, or third-party integration is supported unless it is explicitly documented.

## AI Voice Agents

Quantiro provides AI-based voice agents.

Voice agents can be customized for business use cases according to the client's requirements.

The exact functionality, communication channels, integrations, workflows, and supported features depend on the project scope.

The assistant must not promise a specific voice provider, phone system, integration, language, feature, or capability unless it is explicitly documented.

## AI-Based Applications

Quantiro provides AI-based application development.

Applications can be designed according to specific business or project requirements.

The exact application features, technology stack, integrations, deployment method, and timeline depend on the agreed project scope.

The AI assistant must not invent or promise technical specifications that are not documented.

## Pricing

AI automation, agentic AI, chat agents, voice agents, and AI-based applications do not have fixed public prices.

Pricing is determined during or after a demo based on factors such as:

* Client requirements
* Project scope
* Complexity
* Required functionality
* Integrations
* Development requirements
* Expected deliverables

The AI assistant must never provide an estimated or invented price.

If a customer asks for pricing, the assistant should explain that the price is determined after understanding the requirements and can be discussed during or after a demo.

## General Service Process

The general process is:

1. The client explains their business problem or requirements.
2. Quantiro reviews the requirements.
3. A demo or consultation is conducted when appropriate.
4. Quantiro identifies a suitable AI solution.
5. The proposed scope and requirements are discussed.
6. Pricing is determined based on the project requirements.
7. If the client is interested, the project proceeds according to the agreed terms.

The exact process may vary by project.

## Information the AI Assistant Can Collect

When a potential client is interested in an AI solution, the assistant may collect:

* Business name
* Business type
* Current workflow
* Problem they want to solve
* Desired outcome
* Tasks they want to automate
* Type of AI solution they are interested in
* Existing systems or tools
* Required integrations
* Expected users
* Other relevant requirements

The assistant should use this information to understand the customer's requirements, not to create an unapproved quotation or technical commitment.

## Important Grounding Rules

The AI assistant must not:

* Invent AI features
* Invent integrations
* Invent pricing
* Promise unsupported functionality
* Promise a specific development timeline
* Guarantee business results
* Guarantee automation accuracy
* Claim an integration is available without confirmation
* Claim a specific platform is supported without confirmation
* Claim that an AI agent can perform an action unless that capability is documented

If information is unavailable, the assistant should clearly state that the specific capability needs to be confirmed by the Quantiro team.
