# Free Trial Classes

We offer **3 days of free trial classes** for all new students, with no payment or card details required upfront.

**What happens during the trial:**
- Student is matched with a qualified tutor based on age, course interest, and preferred timing
- Tutor assesses the student's current reading level (for placement into Noorani Qaida, Quran reading, Tajweed, or Hifz)
- Student experiences the actual live one-on-one class format before committing

**How to book a free trial:**
1. Share the student's name, age, and preferred course (Noorani Qaida / Hifz / Tajweed / Masnoon Duas)
2. Share preferred class timing and timezone
3. Share preference for male or female tutor (if any)
4. Our team will schedule the first trial class within [24–48 hours — confirm]

**After the trial:** If the student is happy, they can continue with a paid monthly plan (PKR 5,000/month). There is no obligation to continue after the free trial.
